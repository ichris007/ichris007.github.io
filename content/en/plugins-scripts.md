Not a programmer, but I love to tinker. Sharing practical scripts, CSS snippets, and plugins that I've created and modified.

## Vaults

- [Lifein OS](https://github.com/ichris007/Obsidian_Lifein)（Chinese）
	- Lifein is an Obsidian-based sample vault for a "Productivity System & Life Management System."— a real-world example of how to organize your work and life in one place.
- [Lifein ATS](https://github.com/ichris007/Obsidian_Lifein_ATS) （Chinese）
	- Lifein ATS is a fully local, knowledge-driven candidate management system — built on Obsidian to help recruiters manage candidates without relying on cloud-based tools.
- [Habit Tracker](https://github.com/ichris007/Obsidian-Habit-Tracker)
	- A habit tracker vault running in the Obsidian Datacore plugin that saves daily habit records directly in the frontmatter of your daily notes, providing a visual tracking interface.
## Plugins

- [Jot - Quick Note](https://github.com/ichris007/obsidian-jot-plugin)
	- A card‑style note‑taking plugin for Obsidian, inspired by Andrej Karpathy’s append‑and‑review note.
	- It helps you capture fleeting thoughts, jot down ideas, and manage fragmented notes with ease. Features like nested tags, file attachments, a calendar overview, full‑text search, and seamless Markdown rendering make it a perfect fit for your Obsidian workflow.
- [Birthday Reminder](https://github.com/ichris007/Obsidian-Birthday-Reminder)
	- An Obsidian plugin designed to help you easily manage important birthday information for friends, family, colleagues, and clients. The plugin supports Gregorian/Lunar calendar conversion, zodiac and Chinese animal sign display, intelligent calendar view, and automatically adapts to sidebar and main window layouts.
- [Quick Toggle Plugin](https://github.com/ichris007/obsidian-quick-toggle-plugin)
	- Switch plugins in seconds, without digging through settings.
	- Select your frequently used plugins from a customizable list.
